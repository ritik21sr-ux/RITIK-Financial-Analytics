# Forecast methodology

## Basis

The model uses synthetic FY2025 budget and actual records in INR. Each department has one revenue category and four expense categories. The data generator creates a visible range of variances, including one illustrative Marketing overspend.

## FY2026 forecast

For each month, department, and category:

`FY2026 forecast = same-month FY2025 actual × (1 + category growth assumption)`

Growth assumptions are editable on the `Assumptions` sheet. This method retains the prior year's monthly pattern while applying a flat annual category growth rate.

## Variance convention

- Revenue favorable variance = actual revenue − budgeted revenue.
- Expense favorable variance = budgeted expense − actual expense.
- Contribution favorable variance = actual contribution − budgeted contribution.
- Contribution = revenue − expenses.

Positive favorable variance is favorable. Percent variances are unavailable when the corresponding budget denominator is zero.

## Limits

The forecast is a compact driver-based planning example. It does not model volume and price separately, headcount, hiring dates, working capital, cash flow, taxes, debt, or financial statements. Assumptions are illustrative rather than approved guidance.
