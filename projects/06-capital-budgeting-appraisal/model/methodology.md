# Capital-budgeting methodology

## Free-cash-flow schedule

For Years 1–5, the model calculates:

`FCF = EBIT − cash taxes + depreciation − maintenance capex − change in net working capital`

`EBIT = revenue × gross margin − operating expense − straight-line depreciation`

Year 0 includes initial project capex and the first-year working-capital investment. Revenue compounds from the Year 1 assumption. Year 5 releases the modelled working-capital balance. Interest and financing are excluded, so the cash flows are unlevered.

## Valuation outputs

- **NPV** is the sum of Year 0 free cash flow and Years 1–5 free cash flow discounted at the selected scenario's discount rate.
- **IRR** is calculated from the same Year 0–5 cash-flow series.
- The sensitivity grid changes the Base-case revenue level and discount rate while holding the remaining Base assumptions constant.

## Limitations

The model does not include terminal value, inflation, debt, foreign exchange, tax-loss carryforwards, asset disposals, or probabilistic risk. The simple working-capital convention and straight-line depreciation are illustrative. Real capital requests require approved assumptions, tax treatment, and review by the responsible finance team.
